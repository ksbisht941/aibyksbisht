# Understanding LLM Fine-Tuning

Training large language models from scratch is prohibitively expensive. Fine-tuning offers a practical alternative.

## Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT)
Techniques like LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) allow developers to train massive models on consumer hardware by updating only a small subset of parameters.

## Applications
From creating specialized coding assistants to customer support bots, fine-tuning unlocks the true potential of foundational models.
