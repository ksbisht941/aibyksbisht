# Scaling Microservices Architecture

Transitioning from a monolithic application to a microservices architecture brings flexibility, but scaling it requires careful planning.

## Service Discovery
As the number of services grows, implementing robust service discovery mechanisms becomes crucial for seamless communication.

## Handling Data Consistency
Distributed transactions are notoriously difficult. Event-driven architectures and the Saga pattern offer viable solutions to maintain eventual consistency.
