# The Rise of Agentic Workflows

We are moving from conversational AI to agentic AI—systems that can plan, execute, and evaluate complex multi-step tasks.

## Tool Use
Modern agents are equipped with the ability to interact with external APIs, databases, and file systems, turning them from passive responders to active participants.

## Multi-Agent Systems
By orchestrating multiple specialized agents, organizations can tackle intricate problems that require diverse skill sets.
